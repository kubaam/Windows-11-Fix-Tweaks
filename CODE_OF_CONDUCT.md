# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a respectful, welcoming, and productive environment for all contributors and maintainers, regardless of background, identity, or technical experience.

## Standards

- **Constructive Engagement:** Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- **Respectful Discourse:** Be open to differing viewpoints and deliver critique courteously.
- **Collaboration:** Help new contributors learn the codebase and testing standards.
- **Zero Tolerance:** Harassment, derogatory comments, or unwelcome behavior will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer (@kubaam) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers will take appropriate action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior, including editing comments, closing issues, or blocking accounts if necessary.

To report an issue privately, contact jakubambrus@gmail.com.
